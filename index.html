Drive Backup Access

A private tool Brian McGee uses to back up and read his own Google Drive accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

Drive Backup Access only connects to Google Drive accounts owned by Brian McGee, with his consent. Files are copied only to computers he owns. No data is shared with, sold to, or used by anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising. Contact: 1bsmcgee@gmail.com.
